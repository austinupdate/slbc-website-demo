IRA Charitable Distributions

A tax-smart way to give if you are 70½ or older.

← Back to Giving

If you are 70½ or older, a Qualified Charitable Distribution (QCD) lets you give directly from your IRA to Sugar Land Baptist Church. The gift counts toward your Required Minimum Distribution (RMD) and is excluded from your taxable income, which is often the most tax-efficient way to give.

How It Works

1

Be 70½ or Older

QCDs are available to IRA owners age 70½ and up. If you are 73+, they can also satisfy your annual Required Minimum Distribution.

2

Direct the Transfer

Ask your IRA custodian to send the gift directly from your IRA to Sugar Land Baptist Church. The funds must go straight to the church, not to you first.

3

Give Tax-Free

The amount is excluded from your taxable income and counts toward your RMD. You give more, and you may lower your tax bill.

Benefits

For You

  • Satisfy your Required Minimum Distribution
  • The gift is excluded from your taxable income
  • May reduce taxes on Social Security and Medicare premiums
  • Benefit even if you do not itemize deductions

For SLBC

  • Receives the full gift amount for ministry
  • Often results in larger gifts than cash
  • Reliable support from faithful, long-term givers

Things to Consider

  • The distribution must go directly from your IRA custodian to the church to qualify.
  • Annual QCD limits apply and are set by the IRS. Your custodian or advisor can confirm the current amount.
  • QCDs apply to traditional IRAs. Other retirement accounts may have different rules.
  • Check with your tax advisor to confirm the benefit for your situation.
