Non-Cash Assets

Give beyond the checkbook and make every dollar go further.

← Back to Giving

Some of the most powerful ways to give do not involve your checkbook at all. Gifts of appreciated stock, mutual funds, cryptocurrency, or donor-advised funds can increase your impact while reducing the taxes you would owe if you sold those assets first. In many cases you avoid capital gains tax and still deduct the full value of your gift.

Ways to Give Non-Cash Assets

Appreciated Stock

Donate stock you have held more than a year and avoid capital gains tax entirely, while deducting the full market value.

Mutual Funds

Give shares of appreciated mutual funds directly, with the same tax advantages as individual stocks.

Cryptocurrency

Give Bitcoin, Ethereum, or other major cryptocurrencies. Like stock, appreciated crypto avoids capital gains and is deductible at fair market value.

Donor-Advised Funds

Already have a DAF at Fidelity Charitable, Schwab, or similar? Recommend a grant to SLBC and let your fund handle the paperwork.

An Example

Here is how giving appreciated stock compares to selling it first. Illustrative only.

Giving stock vs. giving cash

  • By giving the stock directly instead of selling it, the giver avoids the capital gains tax on the $15,000 of growth.
  • They still receive a charitable deduction for the full $25,000 market value.
  • SLBC receives the full value of the gift to put to work in ministry.

Benefits

For You

  • Avoid capital gains tax on appreciated assets
  • Deduct the full fair market value of your gift
  • Give more than you could with cash for the same out-of-pocket cost
  • A simple transfer handled by your broker or provider

For SLBC

  • Receives the full value of the asset for ministry
  • Opens the door for larger, more meaningful gifts
  • Strengthens the church's long-term financial health

How to Transfer

Stock and mutual fund gifts are made by electronic (DTC) transfer from your brokerage to the church's account. Contact Kary Gutierrez for our transfer instructions and account details. Most transfers complete within a few business days. For cryptocurrency or donor-advised fund gifts, reach out and we will walk you through the steps.

Things to Consider

  • To receive the full tax benefit, give assets you have held for more than one year.
  • Do not sell the asset first. Transfer it directly so the capital gains tax is avoided.
  • Your deduction is based on fair market value at the time of the gift.
  • Check with your tax advisor about how these gifts apply to your situation.
