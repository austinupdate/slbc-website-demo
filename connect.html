Adult Bible Fellowships

Sunday groups for every life stage: 8:30 AM & 11:05 AM

Our Adult Bible Fellowship (ABF) groups are structured for us to connect with others no matter the life stage we are in. ABF groups meet on Sundays before and after Worship at 8:30 & 11am.

During the school year, mid-week studies meet each week. Whether you’re newly married, raising teenagers, or an empty nester: there’s a group that fits.
