Make Your Generosity Go Further

These options can maximize your impact while providing tax benefits. You don't need to itemize to benefit from some of these.

Popular

Appreciated Securities

Donate stocks or mutual funds directly instead of selling first. You avoid capital gains tax AND receive a deduction for the full market value. A $10,000 stock with a $3,000 basis saves you ~$700 in capital gains taxes vs. selling and donating cash.

IRA Required Minimum Distributions

If you're 70½+, you can direct up to $105,000/year from your IRA directly to SLBC. It satisfies your RMD without increasing your taxable income. Often the most tax-efficient way to give.

Donor-Advised Funds

Already have a DAF (Fidelity Charitable, Schwab, etc.)? Recommend a grant to Sugar Land Baptist Church. Fast, simple, and your DAF handles the paperwork.

Cryptocurrency

Donate Bitcoin, Ethereum, or other crypto directly. Like stocks, donating appreciated crypto avoids capital gains and provides a deduction for fair market value.

Common Questions

Is my gift tax-deductible?

Yes. Sugar Land Baptist Church is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. All gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. You'll receive an annual giving statement each January for your records.

How do I set up recurring giving?

Click "Give Online Now," create a PushPay account, and select "Recurring" during checkout. You can choose weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly. Adjust or cancel anytime from your PushPay account.

How do I transfer stock to SLBC?

Contact Kary Gutierrez, our Business Administrator, at 281.275.6513 for DTC transfer instructions and our brokerage account details. Most transfers complete in 3-5 business days.

Can I give cryptocurrency?

Yes! Our PushPay integration accepts Bitcoin, Ethereum, and other major cryptocurrencies. Select "Crypto" as your payment method when giving online.

How can I get a copy of my giving history?

Log into your PushPay account to view your full giving history anytime. Annual statements are also mailed each January. Contact the church office if you need assistance.

How do I stop receiving mailing envelopes?

Contact Kary Gutierrez at 281.275.6513. Many members switch to online-only giving and no longer need physical envelopes.
