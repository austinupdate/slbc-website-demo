Charitable Gift Annuities

Retirement income for you today, a lasting gift for tomorrow.

← Back to Giving

A charitable gift annuity is a simple agreement between you and Sugar Land Baptist Church. You make a gift of cash or assets, and in return the church pays you a fixed amount every year for the rest of your life. When the Lord calls you home, whatever remains blesses the ministry of SLBC for generations to come. It is part gift, part income, and it lets you support the church you love while providing for yourself.

How It Works

1

You Make a Gift

Transfer cash, stock, or other assets to fund the annuity. Gifts often start around $10,000 or more.

2

You Receive Income

The church pays you (or you and a loved one) a fixed amount every year for life. The rate is based on your age. Older givers receive higher rates.

3

SLBC Is Blessed

After your lifetime, the remaining balance supports the mission and ministries of Sugar Land Baptist Church.

  • Grace receives a guaranteed $3,000 a year for the rest of her life, no matter what the market does.
  • She earns an immediate income tax deduction the year she sets it up.
  • A portion of each yearly payment is tax-free return of principal.
  • Because she used appreciated stock, she reduces and spreads out the capital gains tax she would have owed by selling it.
  • She turns a lump of savings into a lifetime paycheck while making a lasting gift to her church.

Benefits

For You

  • Fixed income for life you cannot outlive
  • An immediate income tax deduction
  • Part of each payment may be tax-free
  • Capital gains tax savings when funded with appreciated assets
  • The joy of a meaningful gift to your church

For SLBC

  • A lasting gift the church can count on
  • Support for ministry for generations to come
  • Deeper, lifelong partnership with faithful givers
  • Resources to further the mission without needing the funds today

Things to Consider

  • A gift annuity is irrevocable. Once established, the gift cannot be returned, though you receive the income payments in exchange.
  • Annuity rates are steady and modest. A gift annuity is a giving arrangement that pays you back, not a high-yield investment.
  • Payment rates depend on your age at the time of the gift. Older givers receive higher rates.
  • We recommend speaking with your financial or tax advisor to see whether a gift annuity fits your situation.
