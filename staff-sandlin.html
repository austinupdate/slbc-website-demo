Dr. Taylor Sandlin

Senior Pastor

Dr. Taylor Sandlin joined Sugar Land Baptist Church in April 2017 as Senior Pastor.

Taylor grew up in Texarkana, Texas. He graduated from Texas A&M University in 2000 with a degree in Speech Communication. In 2004 he earned his Master of Divinity Degree from the George W. Truett Seminary in Waco, TX, and he received his Doctor of Ministry in Worship and Preaching from Truett Seminary in 2013.

Taylor and his wife, Alyson, have two children Sophie and John Curtis.
