What We Believe

We are FOR Jesus. Salvation found only through Jesus Christ.

We are FOR the Bible. Our authority for faith and practice.

We are FOR making disciples. By living in Christian community.

We are FOR living on mission. Taking the Name of Jesus everywhere we go.

We are FOR historic Baptist beliefs. Priesthood of the believer, baptism by immersion, church autonomy, and religious liberty.

THE CHURCH DOES HER BEST WORK WHEN WE PROCLAIM TO THE WORLD WHO WE ARE FOR, NOT WHAT WE ARE AGAINST. SUGAR LAND BAPTIST IS A FAMILY WHO IS FOR ALL THINGS THAT ARE JESUS.

Who We Are

Sugar Land Baptist Church exists to connect people with God, with each other, and with the world. Since our founding as a mission church in the First Colony area of Sugar Land, we’ve grown into a vibrant, multigenerational community united by one purpose: sharing the love of Christ.

Each Sunday at 9:45am we gather for worship. full orchestra, jazz band, choir, soloists, and special ensembles leading us as one body with one voice.

Our Story

Over 40 years of connecting Sugar Land with Christ

1982

Ten families and a single adult are commissioned from First Baptist Church Sugar Land to establish a new Baptist mission. Williams Trace Baptist Church holds its first worship service at Colony Bend Elementary School on February 7.

1984

The church is constituted as an independent church with 300 members under Pastor Ken Corr.

1999

The church relocates to its current 15-acre site on Southwest Freeway under the leadership of Dr. Phil Lineberger, who would serve for 19 years.

2005

The new sanctuary opens with over 2,000 in attendance.

2010

The congregation votes to rename the church Sugar Land Baptist Church.

2017

Dr. Taylor Sandlin is called as Senior Pastor. His first Sunday in the pulpit is Easter, April 16, 2017.

Today

Sugar Land Baptist Church continues to connect people with God, each other, and the world. We are poised and ready for what God will do in the next chapter of our church's great history.
