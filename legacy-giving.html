Legacy Giving

A gift that outlives you, for the church you love.

← Back to Giving

Legacy giving, sometimes called planned giving, is a way to support Sugar Land Baptist Church beyond your lifetime. With a few simple arrangements in your will or on your financial accounts, you can leave a gift that continues the ministry you love for generations, often without affecting the resources you rely on today.

Ways to Leave a Legacy

Bequests

Name Sugar Land Baptist Church in your will or living trust. You can give a specific amount, a percentage, or whatever remains after providing for loved ones.

Beneficiary Designations

Name SLBC as a beneficiary of a life insurance policy, IRA, or retirement account. It is as simple as updating a form with your provider.

Charitable Trusts

Arrangements that can provide income to you or your family for a period of time, with the remainder going to support the church.

Charitable Gift Annuities

Make a gift that pays you fixed income for life, then blesses SLBC. Learn more →

Sample Bequest Language

Adding SLBC to your will can be as simple as sharing this wording with your attorney:

"I give and bequeath ______ (a specific amount, a percentage, or the remainder of my estate) to Sugar Land Baptist Church, Sugar Land, Texas, for its general purposes."

Your attorney can tailor this to your wishes, including directing the gift to a specific ministry.

Benefits

For You

  • Leave a meaningful gift without affecting your finances today
  • Retain full control of your assets during your lifetime
  • Possible estate tax benefits for your heirs
  • The joy of leaving a lasting spiritual legacy

For SLBC

  • A lasting foundation for future ministry
  • Support that carries the mission to the next generation
  • Stability that allows for long-term vision and planning

Things to Consider

  • A will or beneficiary designation can be updated at any time during your lifetime.
  • We recommend working with an estate attorney or financial advisor to put your plans in place.
  • Let us know if you have included SLBC in your plans so we can thank you and ensure your wishes are honored.

Interested in Learning More?

We would be glad to talk through the options and help you and your advisors put a plan in place. There is no obligation, and all conversations are confidential.
